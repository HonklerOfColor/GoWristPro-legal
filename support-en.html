Supported cameras

Can be used with GoPro® HERO9 Black, HERO10 Black, HERO11 Black, HERO11 Black Mini, HERO12 Black, HERO13 Black and MAX 2 cameras. GoWrist Pro requires watchOS 26 or later and no iPhone app.

How do I pair my cameras?

In GoWrist Pro, tap “Pair Camera”; for more cameras, swipe left and choose “Pair New Camera”. On the camera: Preferences → Connections → Connect Device → GoPro Quik App. Keep the camera close to your watch. You pair each camera only once.

The camera is not found.

Check that the camera is in pairing mode (see above) and that Bluetooth is on on your watch (Control Center). Allow GoWrist Pro to use Bluetooth on your watch in Settings → Privacy & Security → Bluetooth.

Which cameras receive the commands?

Only the selected ones. In Settings under “Rig Cameras” you switch each paired camera on or off. Start, stop, mode, arm, sleep and the control only go to these cameras.

Do all cameras start at the same time?

Apple Watch keeps only a few Bluetooth connections at once (typically 2). Up to that number, your cameras start together. With more cameras, GoWrist Pro starts them in a quick wave, one after another, with a short delay per camera. Keep the display on until all of them have confirmed.

“Not started: …”

These cameras did not confirm the start. The others keep recording. Tap Retry or check that the camera is switched on, nearby and has an SD card with free space.

“May still be recording”

The connection dropped while recording, or the stop was not confirmed. GoWrist Pro never stops a recording by itself. Tap Stop again. In the camera's details you can stop it on its own; if it cannot be reached, stop it at the camera and take it out with “Remove from Rig”.

Numbers and positions

In Settings → “Label Cameras” or in a camera's details you give it a number (for example matching a sticker) and a mount position such as Helmet or Handlebar. This tells identical cameras apart.

Arming

The shield button at the bottom left wakes every selected camera, matches the mode and reads battery and storage, without recording.

Putting cameras to sleep and waking them

The button at the bottom right puts the selected cameras to sleep to save their battery. The same button wakes them again without recording; starting a recording also wakes them. A sleeping camera can be woken from the watch for about 8 hours, after that only with its button. GoWrist Pro never wakes a camera by itself.

“The camera no longer knows this watch.”

The camera has deleted the pairing. Remove it in GoWrist Pro in Settings (swipe left in the list) and pair it again.

“Firmware is too old.”

Update the camera with the GoPro Quik app.

Action Button and Control Center

Add the “Toggle Recording” control to Control Center or the Smart Stack. On Apple Watch Ultra you can assign it to the Action Button in the Settings app. It opens GoWrist Pro and starts or stops all selected cameras.

Complication

Touch and hold the watch face, tap Edit and choose GoWrist Pro’s “Rig Status” complication. It shows the last known state of the rig, for example “3/4 REC”.

Is there a live preview?

No. Live preview and media transfer are not available over Bluetooth; the cameras provide those features over Wi-Fi or USB.
